Temu is quietly reallocating traffic toward sellers who carry their own
inventory and price their own listings. The data is unambiguous; the decision
is not.

## What happened

Across 268 Temu stores in our panel, semi-managed sellers received 2.3x the
impressions of fully-managed sellers operating in the same categories during
Q3 2026. Conversion rates were roughly equal. The gap is allocation, not
merchandising.

## Why now

Fully-managed worked while Temu was buying share with price. It cannot scale
past a certain SKU count without the platform carrying inventory risk and
working capital. Semi-managed pushes both onto the seller — which is exactly
why the platform is willing to pay for it in traffic.

## So what

Most sellers assume semi-managed is simply "better". It is better for sellers
with cash flow and a real supply chain; it is worse for anyone treating Temu
as a liquidation channel.

The math on a $6.00 retail unit: fully-managed nets roughly $1.35 after
platform pricing and returns. Semi-managed, priced by the seller at $7.20,
nets roughly $1.90 — but requires about 45 days of working capital per cycle
and full returns exposure.

## For you

- **New sellers:** stay fully-managed until you can fund two inventory cycles.
  Traffic you cannot fulfil is worse than no traffic.
- **Brand sellers:** move your hero SKUs to semi-managed for pricing control,
  and leave the long tail where it is.
- **Arbitrage sellers:** this is the single best fit. You already hold
  inventory and you already price daily.
