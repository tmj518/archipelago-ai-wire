Anti-dumping cases are decided on paperwork long before they are decided on
economics. The exporters who lose are the ones who treat the questionnaire as
an administrative task.

## What happened

The European Commission opened an anti-dumping investigation into cellulose
ethers originating in China, with an investigation period running April 2025
to March 2026 and an injury-assessment period back to 2022. Interested parties
have 30 days from publication to make themselves known and request a
questionnaire.

## Why now

EU import volumes in this chemistry family rose sharply through 2025 while
average unit values fell. That combination — volume up, price down — is the
standard trigger for a dumping complaint, and domestic producers filed
accordingly.

## So what

Most exporters assume their margin level proves they are not dumping. The
Commission tests against normal value in the home market and constructed
value, not against your invoice margin. Documentation quality decides the
individual duty rate; silence means the residual rate applies to you.

On a 20-tonne shipment of a mid-grade ether at €2,400 per tonne, the gap
between an individual rate of 12% and a residual rate of 46% is roughly
€16,300 per container. That is the entire margin on the order.

## For you

- **New exporters:** do not ignore the notice because you ship small volumes.
  Non-cooperation rates apply regardless of volume.
- **Brand sellers with EU distribution:** map which of your EU SKUs contain
  these inputs, and check your supplier's response status in writing.
- **Arbitrage sellers:** treat this as a pricing signal — EU buyers will hedge
  before provisional duties land.
