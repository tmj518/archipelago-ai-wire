Most sellers read yesterday's announcement as good news. It is better than
good news for importers of toys, small appliances, baby products and holiday
gift sets — and worse than it looks for anyone who already booked Q4 freight
against the old rate.

## What happened

The Ministry of Commerce confirmed on September 29 that roughly 90% of the
goods covered by the 2025 targeted tariff list return to MFN rates, effective
October 1, 2026. The adjustment covers an estimated $30 billion in annual
two-way trade. Toys under HS 9503, small household appliances under HS 8516
and baby-care items under HS 9615 are named explicitly in the bulletin.

For an importer, the change is mechanical: a $10 FOB toy that carried a 25%
targeted rate now clears at the 0% or near-0% MFN column. That is $2.50 per
unit, and roughly $30,000 on a single 40-foot high-cube container at 12,000
units.

## Why now

This is the third step of a sequence that started in late 2024. Coal
procurement commitments were locked in first, then agricultural purchase
volumes, and consumer-goods tariff relief was the last domino. Once the
procurement side was signed, the tariff side became a scheduling question
rather than a negotiating one.

## So what

Most sellers assume the saving lands in their pocket. It does not — not
immediately. Three things absorb it first:

| Line item | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FOB unit cost | $10.00 | $10.00 |
| Duty at US customs | $2.50 (25%) | $0.00 (MFN) |
| Ocean freight, per unit | $1.15 | $1.15 |
| **Landed cost per unit** | **$13.65** | **$11.15** |

That 18.3% reduction in landed cost is real, but the platforms are watching the
same announcement. Expect referral-fee or ad-cost pressure to eat part of it
within two quarters. The sellers who keep the margin are the ones who
renegotiate factory pricing now, while factories are still hungry for Q1
volume.

## For you

- **New sellers:** do not chase the tariff story as a product strategy. Use
  the window to place a small test order in a category you can actually
  support with ad spend.
- **Brand sellers with existing ASINs:** reprice selectively, not across the
  board. Take the margin on the top three SKUs and hold price on the rest.
- **Arbitrage sellers:** this is your quarter. Land before the November
  freight peak, and clear inventory before the fee changes land.
