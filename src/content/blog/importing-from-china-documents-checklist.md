## AI Summary

Six documents carry every import from China, and three dated rules now decide whether a missing one costs money. Electronic origin data has been accepted under RCEP since 1 June 2026 and the manual fallback closes on 30 November 2026, after which clearing early means lodging a duty guarantee. Container suitability results last 21 days from 1 October 2026, with statutory fines reaching 100,000 yuan per container. On the destination side, 58 per cent of the listings the EU examined this year were missing identification the law requires.

## What happened

A container does not move because the goods are ready. It moves because the paperwork is complete, and the paperwork has become dated in three places this year.

| Document | Issued by | What it decides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial invoice | Supplier | Declares value, description and terms of sale |
| Packing list | Supplier | Maps cartons, weights and dimensions to the shipment |
| Bill of lading or air waybill | Carrier or forwarder | Title document; without it the goods cannot be released |
| Certificate of origin | Chamber of commerce or authorised body | Whether preferential duty applies at all |
| Export declaration | Supplier, filed with Chinese customs | Evidence the goods left China legally |
| Product certificate | Testing body, per destination market | Whether the goods can legally be sold where they land |

The first four are non-negotiable on every consignment. The last two depend on the product and the destination, and both have moved this year in ways that turn a late document into a payable amount.

**Origin data is electronic now.** Since 1 June 2026, four document types are accepted as electronic origin data — certificates of origin, back-to-back certificates, ACFTA certificates and movement certificates — under GACC Announcement No. 74 of 2026. The manual route has been running as a transition measure and **closes on 30 November 2026**. From 1 December 2026, an importer who wants goods released before that electronic data is confirmed applies for a duty guarantee instead of waiting.

**Container suitability has a clock.** GACC Announcement No. 136 of 2026, effective 1 October 2026, sets the validity of a passed container suitability result at 21 days from the date the result is issued. The exporting producer confirms four self-check items on the declaration. The statutory maximum fine for using a container that failed the check is 100,000 yuan per container, with a further 200,000 yuan available under the same regulation.

> **Duty guarantee** — a deposit lodged with Chinese customs to release goods before the origin data that determines their preferential rate has been confirmed. It is refundable, and it is not free: the amount is tied up for as long as the confirmation takes, so the cost to the importer is the funding cost of that amount over that period rather than the amount itself. Under Announcement No. 74 of 2026 it becomes the route to an early release from 1 December 2026.

## Why now

Three dates converge on the same quarter.

The origin-data transition closes on **30 November 2026**. The container suitability window has been running since **1 October 2026**. And the EU's product-safety regime has been applicable since **13 December 2024**, with an enforcement sweep already completed — May to July 2026, about 1,700 listings across 35 marketplaces, producing 560 enforcement orders to marketplaces.

**The pattern is the same in all three places, and it is not about new documents.** Every one of these documents existed before this year. What changed is that the fallback was withdrawn: the manual origin route closes, the suitability result expires, and the identification requirement is enforced rather than stated. **A missing document used to be a delay. It is now a cost with a date attached.**

## So what

The document set is cheap. Failing it is not, and the arithmetic is worth doing before the container is booked rather than after.

**The origin guarantee, worked.** Take a $60,000 shipment at a 10 per cent duty rate. The guarantee is $6,000. If the confirmation takes 45 days and the money costs 12 per cent a year:

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Shipment value | $60,000 |
| Duty rate on the worked example | 10 per cent |
| Guarantee lodged | $6,000 |
| Days tied up | 45 |
| Funding cost of the guarantee | $88.77 |
| As basis points of shipment value | 14.8 |

**Fourteen point eight basis points is not the point.** The $88.77 is the *smallest* consequence of the origin document being late. The larger one is that a preferential rate which was never confirmed is a rate that was never applied, and that gap is measured in percentage points of the whole shipment, not in basis points of a deposit.

**The container fine, worked.** 100,000 yuan per container is the statutory maximum. At 6.6921 yuan to the dollar that is about $14,900 — **roughly a quarter of a $60,000 consignment, on one container, for a check whose result is valid for 21 days.** The suitability result is not a formality; it is the cheapest item in the file.

**The destination certificate, worked.** The EU sweep found 58 per cent of examined listings missing the manufacturer, the EU responsible person or the product identification the law requires, and issued 560 orders. One delisted listing selling 40 units a day for 30 days loses $13,495.50 in contribution margin on the site's own takedown model. **That failure happens after the goods have cleared and sold.**

## For you

- **First shipment:** ask the supplier for the invoice and packing list in draft before production finishes. At that stage an error is a correction; after the vessel sails it is a delay.
- **Anyone shipping under RCEP or ACFTA:** confirm now which of the four accepted document types your supplier files, and whether they can file electronically before 30 November 2026. The manual route is what closes.
- **Anyone booking a container of food or other suitability-checked cargo:** the 21-day clock starts when the result is issued, not when the container is stuffed. A result obtained early is a result that expires at the port.
- **Sellers into the EU:** the product-safety identification is a listing requirement as well as a product one. The sweep examined listings, not warehouses, and 560 orders went to marketplaces. For the marketplace side of the same regime, see [the GPSR sweep and the 560 orders](/blog/eu-gpsr-sweep-2026-marketplace-listings/).
- **Any importer comparing an FOB quote with a CIF quote:** the Incoterm decides which leg you own, and the certificate obligations follow the term rather than the price. ## FAQ

### Which documents do I need on every shipment?

Four, on every consignment regardless of product: the commercial invoice, the packing list, the bill of lading or air waybill, and the certificate of origin. The certificate of origin is the one that decides money rather than movement, because without it preferential treatment is not applied. Beyond those four, the export declaration is filed by the supplier with Chinese customs, and the product certificate and insurance depend on the destination market and on the Incoterm you agreed with the supplier.

### What changes on 1 December 2026?

The manual fallback for origin data ends. Four document types have been accepted as electronic origin data since 1 June 2026, and the transition window that allowed the older manual route closes on 30 November 2026. From 1 December 2026, an importer who wants goods released before that electronic data is confirmed applies for a duty guarantee instead. The guarantee is refundable, and its cost to you is the funding cost of the amount while it is held rather than the amount itself. The practical question for a buyer is therefore which of the four document types your supplier files, and whether they can file it electronically before the window shuts.

### What is the container suitability check and why does it expire?

It is a customs check on whether a container is fit to carry the goods, under GACC Announcement No. 136 of 2026, effective 1 October 2026. A passed result is valid for 21 days from the date it is issued, and the expiry is the part that catches people: a result obtained early in the packing process lapses before the container reaches the port. The statutory maximum fine for using a container that failed the check is 100,000 yuan per container.

### Do I need EU product certificates if I am not the seller in the EU?

If the goods are sold into the EU, the listing has to identify the manufacturer, the EU responsible person and the product. The 2026 sweep examined about 1,700 listings across 35 marketplaces, found 58 per cent missing that identification, and sent 560 enforcement orders to the marketplaces rather than to the sellers. The obligation attaches to the listing, so a buyer who resells carries it even when the goods cleared correctly on arrival at the border. That is the part importers miss: the border is not where this requirement is enforced, and a clean entry is not evidence that the product file is complete.

### How much does a late document really cost?

Rarely the document. On the origin side the worked example here is $88.77 of funding cost on a $60,000 shipment, which is 14.8 basis points, and that is the smallest consequence — a preferential rate that was never confirmed is a rate that was never applied, and that gap is measured in percentage points of the shipment. On the container side the statutory maximum is 100,000 yuan per container. On the product side it is the margin lost while a listing is down.

## How we calculated this

The guarantee figures apply an annual funding cost pro rata to the amount and the period; the fine is the statutory maximum converted at a dated reference rate.

```text
origin-data duty guarantee
  shipment value            $60,000
  duty rate (assumed)       10%
  guarantee lodged          = $60,000 x 10% = $6,000
  days tied up              45
  annual funding cost       12%
  funding cost              = $6,000 x 12% x 45 / 365 = $88.77
  as basis points           = $88.77 / $60,000 = 0.00148 = 14.8 bp

container suitability fine
  statutory maximum         100,000 yuan per container
  at 6.6921 yuan to USD     = 100,000 / 6.6921 = about $14,900
  as a share of $60,000     = 24.8%

exchange rate
  1 USD = 6.6921 CNY, reference rate dated 9 October 2026
```

Replace the shipment value, the duty rate and the number of days with your own and the guarantee cost follows. The 21-day window and the per-container maximum are the published facts; the dollars depend on your consignment.

## The point

Every document on this list existed before this year. What changed in 2026 is that the fallbacks were withdrawn — the manual origin route closes on 30 November, the container suitability result expires in 21 days, and the product-safety identification is enforced against listings rather than requested.

So the document set is no longer administration. It is the cheapest part of the shipment and the only part where a few days of neglect converts directly into a payment. Build it before you book the space, and check the three dates rather than the list.
