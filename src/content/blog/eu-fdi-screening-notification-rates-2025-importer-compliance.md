Total 2025 notifications hit 667, up from 477 in 2024.

Of all 2025 notified cases, 88% closed within 15 days of submission. The remaining 12% of notified investments required a full in-depth security risk assessment, a 4 percentage point rise over 2024. Across the three consecutive reporting years 2023, 2024 and 2025, the Commission issued formal opinions in less than 2% of all notified cases.

Sector breakdown data shows five high-volume notification categories for 2025: manufacturing at 27% of all transactions, information and communication technologies at 19%, financial activities at 17%, wholesale and retail at 13%, and professional activities at 8%. ## FAQ

**What is the minimum timeline I should build into my EU platform acquisition deal to account for FDI screening?**
For standard low-risk transactions that do not involve sensitive data or critical technology, build a 20-day minimum window from the date you submit a complete notification to the national authority. This covers the 15-day average closure period for 88% of all cases, plus 5 extra days for administrative validation of your submission documents. If your investment touches user personal data, dual-use software, or local government-related infrastructure, extend that window to 45 days to accommodate the full in-depth assessment process that applies to 12% of notified cases. No importer should schedule a deal closing date less than 15 days after notification submission.

**Do I need to submit separate FDI notifications to every EU Member State where my platform operates?**
No. The current mechanism requires you to submit a notification only to the Member State where the target investment entity is legally registered. The EU cooperation framework then handles cross-border information sharing between Member States and the European Commission. Less than 2% of all notified cases result in a formal Commission opinion that requires additional follow-up filings across multiple jurisdictions. This structure will be further streamlined under the 2028 FISR rules, which introduce a single centralized notification portal for cross-border investments that cover multiple EU markets.

**How do the 2025 notification volumes affect my 2028 FISR compliance planning?**
The 40% annual growth rate in notifications confirms that overall filing volumes will continue to rise through 2027 and into the 2028 FISR implementation period. This means national screening authorities will have higher case loads, and importers that delay their notification submissions until the last minute face longer wait times even for low-risk cases. Building a 10% buffer into your current 2027 notification timelines will prepare you for the higher volume environment when the new 2028 rules take full effect, and avoid any backlog-related delays to your investment plans.
