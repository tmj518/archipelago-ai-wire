Headline export growth gets the attention. Bonded logistics growth tells you
what sellers are actually doing with their inventory, which is the more
tradeable information.

## What happened

Throughput at China's bonded logistics centres rose 42.3% year on year in
August 2026, against 6.2% growth in headline export container throughput. The
growth is concentrated in cross-border e-commerce pilot zones.

## Why now

Bonded warehousing lets a seller hold goods inside China, duty-unpaid, until
an order exists. That converts inventory risk into a storage line item.
Platforms pushing 3-day domestic delivery in overseas markets have made
forward-stocking a competitive requirement, and sellers who cannot afford
overseas warehouses use bonded centres as the cheaper staging layer.

## So what

Most sellers assume bonded storage is a tax trick. It is a cash-flow tool.
At roughly $0.55 per cubic foot per month, holding a container for 60 days
costs about $700 — against the working capital saved by not paying duty and
freight forward on unsold units.

I moved a home-goods line to bonded staging in 2024 after two quarters of
over-ordering. It cut my inventory write-downs by about a third, and the
storage bill was less than the write-downs it replaced.

## For you

- **New sellers:** ignore bonded logistics until you have a repeatable
  bestseller. It adds a layer you cannot yet manage.
- **Brand sellers:** use bonded centres to hedge Q1 demand without committing
  to overseas warehouse contracts.
- **Arbitrage sellers:** bonded staging plus a fast air lane is the standard
  play for chasing trend windows.
