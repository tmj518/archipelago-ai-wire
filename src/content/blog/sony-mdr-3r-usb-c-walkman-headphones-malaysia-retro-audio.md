## What happened

Sony has revived its MDR-3-series headphones, the model that debuted in 1979 alongside the Walkman TPS-L2 cassette player. The new version, named the MDR-3R, looks like the original and comes with orange and blue ear cushions, according to an Ars Technica article retrieved on 5 November 2026.

The headline change is the connector. The MDR-3R continues the wired tradition of the MDR-3, but with a USB-C connector instead of the original's analogue plug. USB-C is the oval-shaped port now standard on most phones, laptops and tablets, and it carries both power and data through a single cable.

The sound hardware stays true to the original. Sony says the new headphones use the same 23 mm driver units as the MDR-3 to reproduce sound quality close to the original. A driver is the small speaker inside each ear cup that converts electrical signals into sound.

The MDR-3R also adds two features the 1979 original did not have: an in-line remote control and an integrated microphone. The remote sits on the cable and lets the user answer calls or control playback without touching the connected device.

The retail price at launch is $60 in US dollars. Sony has not announced a Malaysian ringgit price, and the Ars Technica article does not state when or whether the MDR-3R will reach Malaysian stores.
