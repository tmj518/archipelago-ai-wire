You apply for China permanent residence under a specific category, and categories carry different evidence requirements. The National Immigration Administration decides the application, not the local police. Meeting a category is necessary but not sufficient, and a granted permit has a validity period and must be renewed.

## Select your category

Permanent residence applications are made under a named category, not as a general request. Compare the categories and choose the category that matches your actual situation.
Do not switch categories during preparation unless your situation matches the new category and its evidence requirements.

## Prepare category-specific evidence

Each category has different evidence requirements, so prepare the documents stated for your selected category. If a document does not support that category, do not treat it as a substitute.

## Submit and follow the review

Lodge the application through the prescribed channel and provide any further evidence or verification requested. The local police do not decide the application; the National Immigration Administration does.

## Receive the decision and maintain the permit

Even if you meet the category, approval is not guaranteed because the authority makes the decision. If permanent residence is granted, the permit has a validity period, and you must apply for renewal before it expires.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-24; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
