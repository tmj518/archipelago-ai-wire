A foreign driving licence is not exchanged automatically. You must submit a conversion application and usually take a test. Your requirements depend on your residence status and the country that issued your licence.

## Confirm eligibility

Before applying, check that you hold valid residence status meeting the required minimum duration. Your foreign driving licence must also be valid.

## Prepare the licence and translation

A valid foreign licence is required, together with a translation that the local office will accept. Arrange the translation before you submit the application.

## Apply and take tests

Submit the conversion application at the local vehicle management office. The theory test is the common requirement, while a road test depends on the country that issued your licence.

## After approval

Complete any remaining issuance steps after passing the required tests. The office then issues the Chinese driving permit for the approved conversion.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-23; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
