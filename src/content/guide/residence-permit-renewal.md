Your residence permit shows an expiry date. Staying after that date is overstay, even if your job or studies continue. This guide covers where to file, the renewal fee, and the separate risk of working without permission.

## When and where to renew

A China residence permit has an expiry date and must be extended before that date passes.
File the renewal before expiry at the exit-entry authority that issued the permit.
Take your passport, current permit, and the employee or student documents the authority asks to see.

## Fee for one renewal

The fee depends on the validity period of the new permit.
Under one year: 400 yuan per person, per time.
One year (inclusive) to three years: 800 yuan per person, per time.
Three years (inclusive) to five years (inclusive): 1000 yuan per person, per time.

## If the permit expires

Overstaying is penalised per day, and the penalty has a ceiling.
A job or school place does not remove an overstay, and renewal should be completed before the expiry date.
If the date has already passed, report to the exit-entry authority in person and follow its instructions.

## Work permission is separate

Working on a residence permit that does not authorise work is a separate offence from overstaying.
Employees and students should check the permit's work permission before starting any work or internship.
Being allowed to stay does not by itself mean you are allowed to work.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-22; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
