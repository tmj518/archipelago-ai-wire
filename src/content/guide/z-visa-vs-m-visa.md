The Z and M visas cover different reasons for entering China. A Z visa supports employment, while an M visa supports commercial and trade visits only. This guide helps an employee and employer identify the correct route for one application.

## Z visa: employment route

The Z visa is for employment. After the employee enters China, the Z visa is converted into a residence permit. The employer and employee should treat this as an employment-based route, not a general business visitor route.

## M visa: commercial and trade visits

The M visa is for commercial and trade visits. It is not for working, employment, or activities that belong on a Z visa. If the traveler will take up employment in China, the M visa is not the correct choice.

## Required document before a Z visa is issued

A work permit notification is required before a Z visa is issued. The employer should arrange or confirm this document before the employee submits the visa application. The employee should include it with the Z visa application as required.

## Fee for one application

The visa fee is set per nationality by reciprocity, so it is not one number. Because the supplied schedule does not name a nationality, no visa fee is stated here. Before paying, confirm the fee that applies to the applicant’s nationality and requested entry arrangement.

## Confirm before you act

Fees and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-22; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
