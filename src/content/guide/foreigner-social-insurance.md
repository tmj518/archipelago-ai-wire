Foreign employees working in China are covered by the social insurance scheme. Employers must enroll them and pay an employer share for each branch, while employees contribute through payroll. Rates are set locally rather than nationally, and bilateral agreements may exempt some nationals from part of the contribution.

## Coverage

Foreign employees working in China are covered by the social insurance scheme. The employer handles enrollment, while the employee provides needed information and documents.

## Branches and shares

The scheme has several branches. The employer pays a share of each applicable branch, and the employee also contributes a share.

## Local rates

Rates differ by city. A national figure does not exist, so the applicable contribution must be checked locally for the city of employment.

## Bilateral exemptions

Bilateral agreements can exempt some nationals from part of the contribution. Verify the employee’s nationality and agreement status before relying on an exemption.
