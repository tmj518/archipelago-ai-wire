Household goods entering China may fall under unaccompanied baggage, which is a customs category with its own declaration. The price is quoted per cubic metre, and the volume is measured rather than estimated from weight. Door-to-door and port-to-door services are different products, and any figure you receive is a market estimate rather than a published tariff.

## Declare unaccompanied baggage separately

Tell your mover or customs broker that the shipment is unaccompanied baggage. This category uses its own declaration, so do not treat it as ordinary accompanied luggage or a generic cargo shipment.

## Confirm the volume basis

The price is quoted per cubic metre. The chargeable volume is measured from the packed shipment, not estimated by weight, so ask how the surveyor records dimensions and packing.

## Choose the service product

Door-to-door and port-to-door are different products with different prices. State the exact origin and destination points covered by each quote before you compare prices.

## Recognize the quote type

The figure discussed for this move is a market estimate, not a published tariff. Do not present or accept it as an official customs rate or a guaranteed carrier price.
