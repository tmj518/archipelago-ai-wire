This guide covers a tax year for an employee or employer managing Chinese tax residency. The statutory day threshold separates residents from non-residents. The result determines whether income is taxed worldwide or only from China sources.

## What the threshold means

Crossing the statutory day threshold separates a tax resident from a non-resident. The status follows the day count and any applicable treaty or concession rule.

## Period used for counting

Count days within the calendar year. Do not use a rolling annual period, because days are not combined across adjacent calendar years.

## Tax scope after status is set

A tax resident is taxed on worldwide income. A non-resident is taxed only on China-source income.

## Treaty and concession review

Treaty tie-breaker rules can change residency status when the person is treated as resident by multiple jurisdictions. The statutory multi-year concession rule can also change the answer, so a tax professional should confirm its application.
