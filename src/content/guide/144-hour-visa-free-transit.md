This guide explains who can use China’s 144-hour visa-free transit policy as of 22 October 2026. The policy is for passing through to a third country or region, not for a general tourist trip. Before booking, confirm the current eligible nationalities and ports with the National Immigration Administration, because both lists change.

## The transit rule

Your itinerary must be A -> China -> C. You must already hold a ticket to a third country or region, and you cannot return to the country or region you came from.
This is a transit policy, not a tourist visa. It does not allow you to use China as an ordinary holiday destination and then return to your starting point.

## Length and area of stay

The maximum stay is 144 hours, which is six days.
The stay is limited to the region covered by the entry port. It does not authorise travel throughout China as a whole.

## What is not permitted

The stay cannot be extended. It is not permission to work or study.
You may not use it as nationwide entry permission. If your trip requires a longer stay, wider movement, employment, or study, you must make other arrangements.

## Check current eligibility

Both eligible nationalities and eligible ports can change. Do not rely on an old list or a remembered port list.
Check the current information with the National Immigration Administration before you book and again before you travel.

## Confirm before you act

Fees, eligible ports and processing times change. This page was checked on 2026-10-22; confirm the current rule with the authority named in each source above before you rely on a deadline.
